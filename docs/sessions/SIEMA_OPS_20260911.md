## 1. Natro landing-page outage (external, ~00:10-11:00 IST)

Landing pages `siemaexpomorocco.com` and `madesignmaroc.com` (both on
Natro hosting, external to Leena) were down overnight. Meta ads that
route through those landings couldn't hand visitors to Zoho, so the
Zoho webhook to Leena had nothing to receive.

MEASURED — Zoho registrations by expo, comparing 11 Sep to same-clock
prior-night window:

```
                   prior night   11 Sep   Δ
SIEMA (expo 9)         124          3    -121
Madesign (expo 18)      52          5     -47
Public form (all)       —          —     -36 (dip vs baseline)
------------------------------------------
Estimated lost                     ~168
```

Reactivation-source rows stayed flat (13 prior → 12 same window on
11 Sep) — confirming that only the Zoho path was affected. Leena's
webhook endpoint, worker, and DB were healthy throughout.

**Recovery spike**: 40 zoho rows in the 11:00 hour on SIEMA (5-10× the
normal per-hour rate) — Natro came back and either (a) Zoho retried
buffered submissions, or (b) users retried manually. Impossible to
attribute from Leena side.

**HYPOTHESIS**: ~half of the ~168 lost may reappear via retry over the
next 24-72 h. Half is a working estimate; Zoho's retry policy is not
directly measurable from Leena. Ops recipe from `SIEMA_OPS_20260909.md
§3(d)` (Zoho export diff SQL) works here identically if Yaprak wants
to reconcile — same shape, different window
(23:10-10:00 UTC 10-11 Sep).
