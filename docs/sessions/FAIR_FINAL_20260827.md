### Attribution caveats (must state on the SIEMA slide)

1. **`source` is the tag on the visitor row, not a click-through attribution.** People counted under Pixad clicked a Pixad Meta ad → landed on Zoho form → their row was tagged. A person hit by a Pixad ad AND an email cannot be attributed to both.
2. **Reactivation is a list re-marketing channel, not an acquisition channel.** Its 11.46% show rate is on people who came from prior-fair lists — comparable to Pixad only in the sense of "cold-ish list" but the acquisition cost model is entirely different (near-zero variable cost, one-time list procurement).
3. **`Zoho default` (47.28% show) contains organic form fills, PR/press mentions, WhatsApp forwards** — anything that hit the generic Nigeria MP 2026 Zoho form without a specific campaign tag. This is the "unknown-source but definitely engaged" bucket.
4. **`public_form` shows 78% because of survivor bias:** 74% of public_form regs are during-fair walkups. The 30% pre-fair public_form show rate is the honest "public form as pre-registration channel" number; the 95% during-fair is "walk-in registration that immediately checks in" — same-second event, tautologically high.
5. **Pre-reg/Dur-reg split is based on `visitors.created_at` vs 25 Aug 00:00 Lagos.** No adjustment for people who registered Friday before Monday's fair open.

### The 90-second read for Friday

- **Ad spend efficiency (Pixad):** cost X for 4,857 regs → cost X/570 per attendee = **11.7% conversion**. Compare this to whatever the Meta CPM total came to.
- **Email cost efficiency (Email Marketing 28.9% + Zoho default 47.3%):** virtually zero variable cost per send. If SendGrid tier covered it, these are the highest ROI channels by a mile — SIEMA should treat email/organic as first-line and Pixad as fill.
- **Landing Page 23.3% show:** the Meta ad → Landing Page flow underperforms the direct Meta → Zoho form flow. Suggests landing page adds a step without adding conversion.
- **Reactivation 11.5% show** on 1,606 people is 184 attendees for essentially free (list already owned). This is the SIEMA-repeatable pattern.

Total campaign-attributed attendance: **684** (290 + 164 + 230) out of 3,024 unique = **22.6%**.

### Sends-per-attendee (the campaign efficiency number)

- **Campaign 18 (Final Push, single step):** 14,227 sends → 230 attendees = **62 sends/attendee** — best.
- **Campaign 16 (Activate Wave, 3 steps):** 43,466 sends → 290 attendees = **150 sends/attendee**.
- **Campaign 17 (Register Wave, 3 steps):** 78,145 sends → 164 attendees = **477 sends/attendee** — worst by 3×.

Register Wave (cold list, 3-step nurture) was the most expensive per outcome. Activate Wave (warm list of already-registered, 3-step) was 3× more efficient than Register Wave despite similar architecture. Final Push (single tactical send) beat both by another 2–3×.

### 3-sentence SIEMA recommendation

**Prioritize warm-list Final-Push single-send tactics over multi-step nurture flows** — Nigeria's data shows a single well-timed activation email delivered 3× the ROI per send of a 3-step wave. **Register Wave to cold lists is uneconomical at these show rates** (477 sends per attendee at ~$0.001/send is still ~$0.50 CAC on top of list cost — better spend routes to Pixad audiences that match SIEMA's demographic filter). **Keep Activate Wave as the pre-fair backbone** — it's the reliable middle tier and its cadence is what turns registered-but-uncommitted list members into attendees.

### Cert catch-up mailmerge query (ready to use post-B-deploy)

```sql
-- Visitors who registered for a conference topic AND actually attended
-- Ready to feed into a mail-per-topic template loop
SELECT
  v.id AS visitor_id,
  v.email,
  v.name || ' ' || COALESCE(v.last_name,'') AS full_name,
  v.company,
  v.custom_fields->>'conference_topic' AS topic,
  MIN(c.checkin_time) AS first_seen
FROM visitors v
JOIN checkins c ON c.visitor_id = v.id AND c.expo_id = v.expo_id
WHERE v.expo_id = 13
  AND v.custom_fields ? 'conference_topic'
  AND TRIM(v.custom_fields->>'conference_topic') <> ''
  AND v.email IS NOT NULL AND v.email != ''
GROUP BY v.id, v.email, v.name, v.last_name, v.company, v.custom_fields
ORDER BY topic, first_seen;
```

Feed into `POST /api/conference-certificates/…` per row (existing endpoints) or into `email_queue` (Mode 1) with pre-generated cert URLs. Estimated volume: ~340 visitors (attended conference-topic-holders).

**Cleanup note before mailmerge:** there is one row with `topic='Choice One'` and one topic with a typo — flag/normalize during the merge script, don't send a cert for those.
