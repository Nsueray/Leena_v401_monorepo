## 5. Conversion benchmark — and the number that reframes it

**Headline: 2,040 attendees / 12,087 registrations = 16.88%.**

⚠️ **That figure is misleading on its own, and applying it to our 8,471 would be wrong.** Splitting
it changes the picture completely:

| cohort | registered | attended | conversion |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| **Pre-registered** (before day 1) | 10,422 | **986** | **9.46%** |
| **Same-day registrants** (walk-ups) | 1,612 | **1,054** | **65.4%** |

**Half of May's entire attendance — 1,054 of 2,040 (51.7%) — was people who registered on the day
they walked in.** The 10,422-strong pre-registered list delivered only 986 bodies.

### Applied to our position

| scenario | basis | expected unique attendees |
|---|---|---:|
| **Match May, structurally** | 8,441 pre-reg × 9.46% = **799**, plus May's 1,054 walk-ups | **≈ 1,850** |
| Naive (headline rate on today's base) | 8,471 × 16.88% | ≈ 1,430 *(understates — our final registration count will grow with walk-ups)* |
| **Stretch** — pre-reg converts at 12% *and* walk-ups match | 1,013 + 1,054 | **≈ 2,070** |
| **Downside** — pre-reg 9.46%, walk-ups only 75% of May's | 799 + 790 | **≈ 1,590** |

**The realistic band is 1,600-2,050 unique attendees over three days**, against May's 2,040.

**The decisive variable is walk-ups, not the pre-registered list.** We start 2,077 registrations
behind, which costs roughly **200 attendees** at May's 9.46% conversion — recoverable in a single
strong afternoon at the onsite desk. The onsite form is the lever.

## 7. Two structural differences that make this comparison imperfect

**1. 🔴 It is a different fair, and the conference programme is the proof.**
Mega Clima is an HVAC&R technical event with a paid-feel conference: **1,429 conference-type
registrants and 126 conference check-ins on day 1 alone**. Nigeria Mega Project has **100**
conference registrants — a 93% smaller programme. In May the conference was a genuine attendance
driver pulling people through the gate on a schedule; here it is a side room. **Expect a flatter,
less peaked curve** than May's sharp 12:00 spike, because we lack the session-time forcing
function.

**2. 🟠 The lists were built differently, so pre-registered quality is not comparable.**
Ours is **72% more campaign-driven** (1,368 attributed vs 793) and includes 41k+ cold reactivation
recipients mailed three times. May's list was more heavily ads-and-organic. Campaign-sourced
registrants are cheaper to acquire but have **not yet demonstrated they convert to attendance at
the same rate** — at 10:07 exactly **3 of 1,368** campaign-attributed registrants have arrived.
That ratio is the real experiment running today, and it will not be readable until evening.

**A third, smaller caveat:** May's day-1 figures are final and settled; ours are 7 minutes old and
exhibitor-weighted. Every "ahead" in §6 should be re-checked at 18:00 before anyone repeats it.
