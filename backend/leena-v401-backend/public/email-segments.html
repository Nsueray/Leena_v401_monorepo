Email Segments

ℹ️ Help
Email Segments — Send targeted emails to specific groups of registered visitors.
  • All registered — every visitor
  • Checked in — only attendees
  • No-show — registered but didn't attend
  • Different from Send Emails: segments only target THIS expo's visitors
