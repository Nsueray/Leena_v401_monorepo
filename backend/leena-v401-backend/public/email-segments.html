Email Segments

ℹ️ Help
Email Segments — Send targeted emails to visitor groups. Preview count before queueing; sends go through the email worker.
  • Attended (any day) — anyone who checked in
  • Never attended — registered but no check-in
  • Attended on [date] — checked in on a specific fair day (Lagos time)
  • No-show as of [date] — registered by that date, no check-in on or before
  • Unsubscribed and invalid emails are auto-skipped
Email Segmentleri — Ziyaretçi gruplarına hedefli email gönderin. Sıraya almadan önce sayı önizlemesi yapılır; gönderim email worker üzerinden yürür.
  • Attended (any day) — check-in yapan herkes
  • Never attended — kayıtlı ama check-in yok
  • Attended on [tarih] — belirli bir fuar günü check-in yapanlar (Lagos saati)
  • No-show as of [tarih] — o tarihe kadar kayıtlı, o tarihe kadar check-in yok
  • Unsubscribe ve geçersiz emailler otomatik atlanır
