Re-activation Campaigns

ℹ️ Help
Re-activation Campaigns — Invite past visitors to register for the current expo. They receive an email with activation link, click and confirm to register.
  • Upload Excel OR select from previous expo
  • Template must contain {{activation_url}} placeholder
  • Already registered visitors are automatically skipped
  • Token links expire after 30 days
  • Form Design: customize activation page appearance

ℹ️ What does the Wizard do?
Campaign Wizard — turns one uploaded Excel file into two ready-to-send drip campaigns, split automatically by whether each person is already in our database.
  • ACTIVATE wave — people already in our database from any past expo. They get a one-click token link that activates their badge on the target expo. No re-registration needed.
  • REGISTER wave — people NOT in our database at all. They get a link to the registration form.
What it does NOT do: it does not separate people who attended (checked in) from people who registered but did not show up. Both land in ACTIVATE. To message those two groups differently, prepare two Excel files and run the wizard twice.
