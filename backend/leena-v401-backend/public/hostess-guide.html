🔴

Visitor not registered for this session

DEFAULT: Tap Cancel. Direct the visitor to the admin desk. Only Force IF the visitor can show: registration email, ticket, or invitation for this session.
Force cannot be undone. The system permanently records the session in the visitor's record and sends the certificate email.
⚠️

"Not registered for this session" card appeared

Default action: Cancel. Send the visitor to the admin desk. Only proceed with Force if the visitor proves their registration (email, ticket, or invitation).
This card is the second warning. Treat it the same as the Force warning above.
🔍

QR doesn't scan, or "Not Found" appears

  1. Try scanning once more — hold steady, good light.
  2. Still not working? Type the badge ID manually in the input field below the camera.
  3. Still "Not Found"? Take the visitor's email and phone and send them to the lead hostess.
🪪

Visitor wants a badge (new or reprint)

Direct them to the entrance gate. Badges are only printed at the gate scanner.
This scanner is for conference certificates only.
📍

Make sure you are on the conference scanner

Look at the top of your screen. It must say: "Conference Check-in" If it says "Badge Scanner" or "Lead Scanner" — STOP. Open the bookmarked URL for conference. Ask the lead hostess if unsure.
Bookmark / URL: <will be filled by Suer before fair>
📧

Visitor says: "I didn't get the certificate email"

  1. Ask them to check their spam / junk folder. Wait 1-2 minutes.
  2. If still nothing, scan their QR again — a duplicate overlay will appear with a "Resend Certificate" button.
  3. Maximum 2 resends. If still no email after 3 attempts total, send them to the lead hostess.
🆘

Escalate to the lead hostess for:

  • Wrong certificate issued by mistake
  • Visitor data is wrong (name / company / email)
  • Visitor not found in system, but insists they registered
  • Certificate fails to send after 3 resends
  • Scanner is frozen and refresh didn't help
  • Anything you're not sure about
📡

"Connection Error" keeps showing, or device hangs

  1. Wait 30 seconds, try one more scan.
  2. Still not working? Refresh the page. (You'll lose today's running count, but the scanner will work again.)
  3. Wi-Fi still down after 1 minute? Switch to paper: Write down for each visitor:
    Name · Company · Session · Time     Give the paper log to the lead hostess when Wi-Fi returns — entries will be added to the system manually.
